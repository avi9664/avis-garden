Hi, I’m Avi, and I’m interested in energy justice!

Extreme weather is coming and our people and energy infrastructure aren’t prepared for it. I believe that all people, especially marginalized folks, should have access to affordable energy, community, clean air, and sustainable homes that keep people comfortable in a changing climate. 

I’m interested in combining my engineering skills and policy analysis to work on problems related to energy affordability, building decarbonization, or distributed energy resources. I graduated from Carnegie Mellon University in Materials Science with an additional major in Engineering and Public Policy. As a Master’s in Development Engineering at University of California, Berkeley, I’m trained in systems thinking and quantitative modeling, combining analysis of energy policy with geospatial data analysis, life cycle assessments, and/or control-oriented energy system models.
